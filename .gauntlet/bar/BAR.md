## Hard gates

1. Every ticket's original acceptance criteria has observed behavioral evidence. Domain tests alone do not prove a vertical slice.
2. Private verified auth, real isolated development storage, server-side owner checks, idempotent creation and saves, transactional revisions and writer fencing. No mock persistence claimed as cloud sync.
3. All household/creator/care/autonomy/building/economy/neighborhood/relationship/Moo-Moo/pregnancy/generation/aging/death/ghost/business capabilities work through real controls and persist.
4. All confirmed rules: eight living cats including kittens, reserved litter slots, mutual readiness, chance conception, at-capacity Moo-Moo without pregnancy, permanent death, fixed aging, no absent-time advancement.
5. Every core flow works with touch, keyboard and mouse. Accessible readable UI, reachable controls, safe-area handling, no clipped text or horizontal overflow.
6. Art matches the approved pastel cartoon/pixel neighborhood direction through actual sprites, animation, environment and UI. No reference-image gameplay, generic dashboard substitute or dead button.
7. Full applicable lint/typecheck/build/domain/integration/browser/security/save-recovery gates pass for exact candidate commit. Focused checks locally; broad suites in CI or dedicated validation environment.
8. Orchestrator independently dogfoods and captures each material state at desktop and phone sizes. Every screenshot opened and described. Physical-device requirements retain their evidence tier.
9. Fresh reviewers receive requirements, exact source/diff and orchestrator evidence, not builder rationale/transcript/self-grades. Three lenses for load-bearing acceptance: behavior/security, visual/UX, whole-game/regression.
10. Private deployed verification and recipient acceptance remain required for final product completion. Infrastructure/account prerequisites never justify false completion.

## Reproducible captures

Before visual judging: two fresh isolated contexts of identical code, viewport, fixture/seed, fixed simulation step, paused game, settled fonts/assets, deterministic animations. Compare full image bytes/pixels and relevant crops. Investigate any difference; no verdict on nondeterministic evidence. A generative concept is a direction reference, not an exact pixel-identity target. Each implementation capture must itself be deterministic.

## Scoring and loop

Inspect five dimensions, 20 points each: product/simulation fidelity, persistence/security, visual craft to approved reference, desktop/phone interaction, reliability/whole-game cohesion. Each hard gate independently mandatory. Final acceptance requires 100/100 from each independent lens and two consecutive valid rounds meeting the bar; never round up. Until then the product is in progress.

Every round records candidate commit, reproducibility proof, viewed desktop/phone captures, test output, blind-review receipt, observable symptoms, and comparison to last accepted candidate. Return symptoms to AGY for repair. Reject regressions. Same symptom after two ineffective fixes triggers measured root-cause analysis. Three no-improvement rounds park that concern with its precise dependency while other permitted work continues. No fixed total round cap. Only user can lower/change the frozen bar.
