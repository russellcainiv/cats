# Cat Household

The language of a browser game about directing a household of cats with individual personalities and free will.

## Language

**Household**:
The group of up to eight cats the player directs together, including kittens.
_Avoid_: Human family, single player cat

**Cat**:
An individual member of the household with its own personality and free will.

**Free will**:
A cat's capacity to choose actions on its own alongside the player's direction.
_Avoid_: Direct control only

**Moo-Moo**:
An action between cats in love who are both willing and in the mood, carrying a chance of producing kittens. The player or a cat can initiate it, but the other cat's willingness is required in either case.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed pregnancy, separate approval to have kittens

**Ghost cat**:
A deceased cat that occasionally visits the household without returning to life.
_Avoid_: Resurrection, restored living cat

**Memorial**:
A remembrance of a deceased cat within the game.
